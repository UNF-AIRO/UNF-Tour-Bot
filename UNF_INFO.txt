UNIVERSITY OF NORTH FLORIDA - CAMPUS INFORMATION

=== MAIN BUILDINGS ===

Library (Thomas G. Carpenter Library):
- Located in the center of campus
- 3 floors of study and research areas
- Computer labs available
- Extended hours during finals
- Contact: 904-620-2600

Student Center (Osprey Hall):
- Main hub for student activities
- Houses dining facilities, bookstore, and offices
- Rooftop terrace with campus views
- Meeting rooms available for student organizations

Engineering Building:
- Located near the north side of campus
- Home to Computer Science, Civil Engineering, and Mechanical Engineering departments
- Modern labs and research facilities
- Parking available nearby

=== DINING OPTIONS ===

Osprey Dining Hall:
- Main meal plan location
- Multiple food stations
- Open 7am - 8pm on weekdays

Osprey Cafe:
- Quick grab-and-go options
- Located in Osprey Hall
- Open 6am - 8pm

=== CAMPUS FACILITIES ===

Fitness Center:
- Weight room and cardio equipment
- Group fitness classes
- Aquatics center with pool
- Open to students with valid ID

Parking:
- North campus lot: 500 spaces, $3/day visitor
- South campus lot: Free for students
- Visitor parking at main gate
