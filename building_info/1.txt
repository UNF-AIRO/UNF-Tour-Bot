J.J Daniel Hall, Building 1

J.J. Daniel Hall, also known as Building 1, is the main administration building at the University of North Florida (UNF). Opened in October 1972, it was one of the first four original buildings on campus and serves as the university's administrative hub, housing the President's Office and several deans' offices. The building is named for Jaquelin James "J. J." Daniel, a Jacksonville attorney and civic leader who was instrumental in the founding of UNF. 

It currently houses numerous administrative departments, including:
    President's Office and Office of the Provost.
    Dean's Offices for the Coggin College of Business, Brooks College of Health, and other colleges.
    Office of Admissions and the Ticket Box Office.
    Division of Information Technology Services.
    Visitors can find the building at the core of the UNF campus. For parking, green-lined visitor spots are available in Lot 7.
