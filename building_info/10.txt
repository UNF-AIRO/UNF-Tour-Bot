Honors Hall, Building 10

Building 10 is currently known as Honors Hall. It is a smaller, two-story academic building that sits right in the heart of the original campus "core."

Official Name: Honors Hall
Primary Function: Primarily houses offices for the Hicks Honors College and general university classrooms.

Key Departments & Services
    While it is named Honors Hall, it serves a few different administrative and academic purposes:
    Hicks Honors College: This is the administrative home for the Honors program, including advising and faculty offices.
    Classrooms: The building contains several smart classrooms used by various departments across the College of Arts and Sciences.
    The Writing Center: For a long time, the UNF Writing Center was a staple of this building, though specialized tutoring services occasionally shift to the Library or nearby halls.

Historical Significance
    Construction: Completed in 1974-1975 as part of the Phase II campus expansion.
    The "Link" Building: Historically, Building 10 has served as a physical and functional link between the larger classroom blocks of Buildings 9 and 11.
    Naming: It was officially designated as Honors Hall in the mid-2000s to give the growing Honors program a dedicated identity on campus.

Fun Fact: Because it’s smaller than its neighbors (9 and 11), students often find it a quieter place to study between classes compared to the high-traffic areas of the Student Union
