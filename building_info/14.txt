Andrew A. Robinson Jr. Building, Building 14

Building 14 is formally known as the Andrew A. Robinson Jr. Building. It is not a single structure but rather a cluster of multi-purpose facilities that form the Andrew A. Robinson Jr. Student Life Center.

Core Details
    Official Name: Andrew A. Robinson Jr. Building
    Components: The complex includes several lettered sections (14A, 14B, 14D, 14E) that house distinct functions.

Key Departments & Sections
    Building 14A (Robinson Theater): A performance venue adjacent to the Fine Arts Center that hosts a variety of university and community performance events.
    Building 14B (Housing and Residence Life): Serves as the main administrative headquarters for UNF Housing, where students go for housing contracts and residence information.
    Building 14D: Contains various academic offices, including the Office of the Dean for the College of Arts and Sciences.
    Building 14E: Houses the Department of Mathematics and Statistics and a specialized mathematics computer lab facility

Historical Significance
    Namesake: Dedicated in 1987 to honor Dr. Andrew A. Robinson Jr., a charter faculty member and the first African-American to serve as UNF's Interim President (1980–1982).
    Expansion: Dr. Robinson passed away in 1988, just two months before a major expansion and renaming of the building.
    Legacy: Before joining UNF, Dr. Robinson was the founding principal of Jacksonville's William Raines Senior High School and a prominent local civic leader.
