Osprey Commons, Building 16

Building 16 is officially known as Osprey Commons. It is the primary student dining hub on campus and serves as a major connection point between the academic core and the residential housing areas

Official Name: Osprey Commons
    Construction: Opened in August 2012. The 26,000-square-foot, $13 million facility was built to LEED Silver Certification standards.
    Primary Function: Centralized dining services and administrative offices for UNF Dining.

Key Departments & Services
    The Osprey Café: This multi-level dining facility is the main feature of the building. It offers 600 seats and features a 28-foot-tall ceiling with six "action stations" where chefs prepare fresh meals.
    The Talon Room: Located on the fourth floor, this is a large banquet-style room used for formal university gatherings and events.
    The Faculty Lounge: Also on the fourth floor, this space is managed by the Office of Faculty Excellence & Academic Engagement as a quiet area for faculty to relax and collaborate.
    Campus Planning, Design and Construction: This administrative department is located in Suite 3200.

Notable Facts
    Scenic Views: The building was specifically designed with extensive glass walls to provide students and staff with picturesque views of Candy Cane Lake while they eat.
    Replacement of "The Café": Before this building opened, the "Old Cafeteria" was widely considered the oldest building on campus; Building 16 modernized the dining experience while staying on the same historical footprint.
