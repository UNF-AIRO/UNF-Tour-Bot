Osprey Clubhouse, Building 17

Building 17 is the Osprey Clubhouse, a 15,000-square-foot multipurpose facility that serves as a major social and service hub for students living on campus.

Core Details
    Official Name: Osprey Clubhouse.
    Primary Function: Student-centered recreation, dining, and essential housing services.

Key Amenities & Services
    The Clubhouse is packed with resources designed for student life:
    Dining: Home to a Pita Pit location on the first floor, which typically operates late into the night.
    Recreation: Features a 3,000-square-foot outdoor pool and a 6,000-square-foot pool deck. Inside, the Game Room offers billiards, ping pong, arcade-style racing, and lounge furniture.

Housing Services:
    Welcome Desk: Staffed by Housing and Residence Life to assist residents.
    Mail Center: Provides access to mailboxes and package delivery for various housing communities.
    Laundry: Houses a large laundry facility primarily used by Osprey Village residents, though accessible to all residents.
    Meeting Spaces: Includes a Multipurpose Room (MPR) on the second floor that can host up to 60 guests for events, as well as student leadership offices for the Residence Hall Association (RHA).

Notable Facts
    Availability: The main facility is often accessible 24 hours a day for students, though specific services like the pool and Pita Pit have set operating hours.
    Tech-Enabled Laundry: The laundry machines are known for their ability to text students when their loads are complete.
