Competition Pool Complex, Building 15

Building 25 is the Competition Pool Complex, a premier aquatic facility that serves both the university's athletic teams and the general student body for recreation. 

Core Details
    Official Name: Competition Pool Complex
    Building Components: The complex is technically split into three designated sections:
        25A: Competition Pool Complex Building A.
        25B: Competition Pool Complex Building B.
        25C: Competition Pool Equipment Building.
    Primary Function: Home to the UNF Women’s Swimming team and a hub for campus recreational swimming. 
    
Key Amenities & Features
    Olympic-Size Pool: Features a 50-meter by 25-yard outdoor heated pool with multiple lanes for lap swimming and competitive meets.
    Locker Rooms: Includes full-service locker room facilities for students, faculty, staff, and visiting athletes.
    Electronic Scoreboard: Equipped with a professional timing system and scoreboard for official NCAA swimming competitions.
    Accessibility: The pool is open to all students with a valid Osprey 1Card during posted recreational hours.

Historical Significance
    Replacement of Older Facilities: This complex serves as the modern replacement for the university's original pool. Before the Student Wellness Complex (Building 61) opened in 2011, many of the fitness functions were centered around Building 25 and the now-demolished Dottie Dorian Fitness Center.
    NCAA Hub: As the primary training ground for the North Florida Ospreys, it has hosted numerous conference championships and regional aquatic events.
