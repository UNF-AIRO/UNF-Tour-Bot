Building 26 at the University of North Florida is officially known as The Field House. It is a major recreational hub that underwent a significant transformation to better serve student life and competitive sports. 

Core Details
    Official Name: The Field House
    Primary Function: Home to Competitive Sports programs, including Intramurals, Club Sports, and Open Recreation. 

Key Features & Facilities
    The 26,000-square-foot facility is designed for high-energy student use:
    Indoor Courts: Features one full-size competition court (east-west) that can be configured into three north-south half-courts for basketball.
    Versatile Sports Space: Supports a variety of sports including volleyball (two courts), indoor soccer, badminton, and pickleball.
    Seating & Capacity: Includes bleachers that can accommodate approximately 450 people, with a total building capacity of 1,280.
    Academic Space: Contains one classroom that seats roughly 20 people for team meetings or small sessions. 

Historical Significance
    The Big Transformation: The building was originally the university's Aquatic Center. In 2015, it completed a $5.1 million renovation to repurpose the space into the current Field House.
    Sustainability: During the renovation, it was designed to meet LEED Silver certification standards, using high-efficiency systems and recycled materials to reduce environmental impact.
    Community Shift: The conversion happened after the university decided to build the new Competition Pool Complex (Building 25) rather than repairing the aging aquatic facility previously at this site. 

Access: The facility is free to use for currently enrolled students with a valid Osprey 1Card.
