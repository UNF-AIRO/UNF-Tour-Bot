Harmon Baseball Stadium, Building 27

Building 27 is officially known as the Harmon Baseball Stadium. It is a cornerstone of the university's athletic facilities, serving as the home field for the UNF Ospreys baseball team.

Core Details
    Official Name: Harmon Baseball Stadium.
    Primary Function: Intercollegiate baseball competition and training facility.

Key Features
    Seating Capacity: The stadium features a grandstand that seats approximately 1,000 fans.
    Fan Amenities: Includes a family picnic area, a concession stand, and a press box.
    Field Capabilities: The facility is equipped with lighting for night games.
    Supporting Structures: It is located next to Building 28A, which is the Batting/Pitching Facility used for player development.

Historical Significance
    Namesake: The stadium is named in honor of Doug and Linda Harmon, longtime supporters of UNF Athletics.
    Community Impact: Doug Harmon was instrumental in starting the university's baseball program and served as the first president of the Osprey Club, a fundraising organization for student-athletes.
    Conference Standing: It is regarded as one of the premier baseball facilities in the Atlantic Sun (ASUN) Conference. 

Pro Tip: During large campus events like commencement ceremonies, Lot 18 (near the stadium) is the recommended parking area due to its high capacity and shuttle access to the UNF Arena.
