Dusty Rhodes Batting Facility, Building 28

Building 28 (specifically 28A) is the Dusty Rhodes Batting Facility. It is a specialized athletic training center that supports the player development of the UNF baseball and softball programs.

Core Details
    Official Name: Dusty Rhodes Batting Facility
    Primary Function: Indoor training, batting practice, and pitching drills for the university's baseball and softball teams.

Key Features
    All-Weather Training: The facility allows student-athletes to conduct hitting and pitching sessions regardless of weather conditions, featuring multiple batting cages and pitching tunnels.
    Proximity to Competition: Its location right next to the baseball stadium makes it the primary "warm-up" hub for players before home games.

Historical Significance
    Namesake: The facility is named after Dusty Rhodes, the legendary founding head coach of the UNF Baseball program. Rhodes led the Ospreys for 23 seasons (1988–2010), amassing over 900 wins and leading the team through its transition from NAIA to NCAA Division II and finally to Division I.
    Legacy: Under Coach Rhodes, UNF became a national powerhouse, reaching the NCAA Division II World Series multiple times. The naming of the training facility honors his massive contribution to building the university's athletic identity.
