UNF Softball Complex, Building 31

Core Details
    Official Name: Softball Complex
    Construction: The field was originally completed in 1992, coinciding with the inaugural season of Osprey softball.
    Primary Function: Intercollegiate softball competition and training facility.

Key Features & Recent Upgrades
    The facility has seen extensive modernization to support the program's record-breaking 2025 season, where the team won the Columbia Regional title:
    Seating: Currently features elevated seating for approximately 500 fans following a 2020 expansion.
    Training Facilities: Includes batting cages located behind the home dugout and a turf "halo" added for improved player safety and aesthetics.
    Press Box: A new ground-level press box was installed after a hurricane destroyed the original elevated structure in 2018.
    Technology: Features a NEVCO videoboard (installed in 2020) and a high-efficiency LED lighting system upgraded in 2022 for night games.
    Dugouts: Both the home and visitor dugouts include built-in restrooms and storage.

Notable Facts
    Shared Amenities: Because of its proximity to Harmon Stadium, fans at the softball complex have easy access to the concession stands and restrooms located in Building 27.
    Community Support: Major facility projects were completed with support from local advocates, including Glenn and the late Anita Ross.
