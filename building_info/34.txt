UNF Arena, Building 34

Core Details
    Official Name: UNF Arena
    Primary Function: Home to the Ospreys' Division I men’s basketball, women’s basketball, and volleyball teams.

Capacity:
    Basketball: Seats approximately 5,800.
    Concerts/Graduations: Can accommodate up to 6,300 with floor seating.

Key Features & Amenities
    The Arena is a versatile complex that underwent significant upgrades in 2022: 
    CSI Companies Court: The main playing surface, named after a local staffing firm.
    Specialized Labs: Houses a Kinesiology Lab and the J. Blacker Gym (a dedicated strength and conditioning room).
    Training Facilities: Includes eight locker rooms and a sports medicine treatment area used by the athletic training program.
    Practice Space: The bottom seating is retractable, allowing the floor to open up into three regulation basketball courts. There are also two half-courts located upstairs.
    Administrative Hub: Home to the Offices of Athletics Administration and various coaching staffs. 

Historical Significance
    Opening: Completed in January 1993 at a cost of roughly $9.2 million.
    Major Events: It has hosted high-profile training camps for the Orlando Magic and served as a training site for the 2004 U.S. Olympic men’s and women’s teams.
    The Osprey Fountain: The landmark fountain in the west courtyard was unveiled in 1994 during the spring graduation ceremony.
    Attendance Record: The highest recorded attendance was 6,155 fans on March 8, 2015, when the Ospreys won the Atlantic Sun Championship.
