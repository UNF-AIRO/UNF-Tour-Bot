Tennis Clubhouse, Building 37

Building 37 is officially known as the Tennis Clubhouse. It serves as the primary support facility for the university’s tennis programs and is a key part of the North Florida athletics corridor.

Core Details
    Official Name: Tennis Clubhouse
    Primary Function: Operations hub for the UNF Men's and Women's Tennis programs

Key Departments & Services
    This building is dedicated to the logistical and administrative needs of the tennis teams:
    Coaching Offices: Houses the offices for the Tennis and Soccer coaches.
    Team Support: Provides locker rooms and meeting spaces for the Osprey tennis athletes.
    Event Hub: Serves as the central check-in and administrative point for tennis tournaments and matches held at the adjacent courts.

Notable Facts
    Strategic Placement: Its location right next to the Arena Parking Garage (38) makes it highly accessible for visiting teams and fans attending matches.
    Athletic Cluster: Building 37 is part of a dense cluster of sports facilities that includes the Harmon Baseball Stadium (27) and the Softball Complex (31), forming the core of the university's eastern athletics zone.
