Arena Parking Garage, Building 38

Building 38 is the Arena Parking Garage, the largest parking structure on the UNF campus. It serves as the primary parking hub for both daily student commuting and major campus events.

Core Details
    Official Name: Arena Parking Garage
    Capacity: Contains approximately 2,200 parking spaces across four levels.

Key Features & Services
    Permit Types: Primarily serves as a Blue Lot (Student) and Orange Lot (Faculty/Staff) facility, though it also includes metered parking for visitors.
    Event Parking: This is the designated parking area for UNF Basketball games, graduations, and concerts held at the Arena.
    Safety Features: Equipped with Blue Light emergency phones on every level and 24/7 security camera surveillance.

Notable Facts
    Construction: Opened in 2006 to alleviate the massive parking shortage caused by the university's rapid growth in the early 2000s.
    The Covered Walkway: The bridge on the third floor allows students to walk directly into the UNF Arena or toward the Student Union without crossing ground-level traffic or dealing with rain.
    Solar Power: The top deck of the garage has been considered for solar panel installations as part of UNF's sustainability initiatives.
