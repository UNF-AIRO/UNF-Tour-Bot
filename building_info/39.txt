J. Brooks Brown Hall, Building 39

Building 39 is J. Brooks Brown Hall, the primary academic and administrative hub for the Brooks College of Health. It serves as a center for excellence in nursing, nutrition, and public health education.

Core Details
    Official Name: J. Brooks Brown Hall
    Purpose: Houses the Dean’s Office for the Brooks College of Health and multiple specialized academic departments.

Key Features & Services
    Academic Departments: Home to the departments of Clinical & Applied Movement Sciences, Nutrition & Dietetics, and Public Health.
    Student Advising: The specialized BCH Advising Office (Room 2031) is located here, providing dedicated support for healthcare majors.
    Renovated Labs: Recently underwent significant interior renovations to modernize nursing and kinesiology laboratories, ensuring students work with industry-standard equipment.

Notable Facts
    The Namesake: Named in 1997 after Dr. J. Brooks Brown, a visionary Jacksonville thoracic surgeon and a key figure in the founding of Memorial Hospital.
    Campus Growth: Originally opened in 1995, the building proved so popular that a 33,000-square-foot "Addition" (Building 39A) was constructed just over a decade later to handle the influx of health students.
    Central Connection: It is strategically located next to the Arena Parking Garage (Building 38), making it one of the most accessible academic buildings for commuting students.

J. Brooks Brown Hall Addition, Building 39A

Core Details:
    Official Name: J. Brooks Brown Hall Addition
    Purpose: Home to Student Health Services and advanced clinical simulation laboratories.

Key Features & Services:
    Student Health Services (SHS): The primary on-campus medical clinic (Room 2098) where students can receive primary care, immunizations, and wellness exams.
    Simulation Labs: Features state-of-the-art clinical simulation suites that mimic real hospital environments, allowing students to practice life-saving skills on high-fidelity mannequins.
    Sustainability: This was one of the first buildings on campus built to LEED-certified standards, reflecting UNF’s commitment to "green" building practices.

Notable Facts:
    Award-Winning Care: The Student Health Services unit housed here has frequently won the Presidential Quality Customer Service Award for its care of the Osprey community.
    Bridge to Success: While technically a separate structure, 39A is physically linked to Building 39, creating a seamless "health complex" in the campus core.
