Skinner-Jones Hall Building 4

Building 4 at the University of North Florida (UNF) is officially known as Skinner-Jones Hall. It is one of the original buildings from the university’s 1972 opening and serves as a major classroom and laboratory hub.

In this building
    First Floor: Department of Construction Management.
    Second Floor: UNF Writing Center (Suite 2501) and School of Computing.
    Third Floor: School of Engineering.
    Fourth Floor: CCEC Dean’s Office and additional Construction Management offices.

Specialized Facilities:
    Writing Center: Located on the second floor, accessible via a spiral staircase from the back of the Bamboo Garden.
    Specialized Labs: Includes the GeoMaterial Prep Laboratory and a Nurse Anesthetist Lab.
    Engineering Labs: Engineering and construction computer labs are located on the second floor in rooms 2305, 2306, and 2307.
    CIRT Video Studio: Features a green screen and lightboard for faculty use (Room 4306).

The building wraps around the UNF Bamboo Garden, a serene courtyard planted in 2009. 
    Meditative Space: It features over 18 species of non-invasive bamboo and is designed with permeable concrete to manage rainwater sustainably.
    Natural Cooling: The south-facing brick walls of Building 4 create a "micro-climate" in the garden, absorbing sun during the day and releasing heat at night.
