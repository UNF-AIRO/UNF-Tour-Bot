Osprey Nest (Baseball), Building 40

Building 40 is the Osprey Nest, a dedicated facility specifically supporting the UNF Baseball program. It is located on the northwest side of campus, integrated into the athletics complex near the baseball stadium

Core Details
    Official Name: Osprey Nest
    Primary Occupant: North Florida Ospreys Baseball coaching staff and student-athletes. 

Key Features & Services
    Coaching Offices: Houses the primary administrative offices for the Baseball coaches, providing a central hub for strategy and recruiting.
    Player Facilities: Includes essential spaces for student-athletes, such as locker rooms and meeting areas, situated directly adjacent to the playing field for quick transition between meetings and practice.
    Strategic Proximity: Located near the Batting/Pitching Facility (Building 28A) and Harmon Baseball Stadium (Building 27), forming a specialized "baseball hub" on campus. 

Notable Facts
    The Athletics Corridor: While many academic buildings are clustered in the campus core, Building 40 is part of the "Athletics Corridor" on the northwest ridge, alongside the UNF Arena (Building 34) and Hodges Stadium (Building 46).
    Game Day Hub: During home games, the proximity of the Osprey Nest to the stadium makes it a highly active area for team operations and player-coach interactions.
