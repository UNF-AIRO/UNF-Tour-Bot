Martin P. Garris Police Building, Building 41

Building 41 is the Martin P. Garris Police Building, the headquarters for the University Police Department (UPD). It serves as the 24/7 central command and communication center for all campus safety operations.

Core Details
    Official Name: Martin P. Garris Police Building
    Primary Occupant: UNF University Police Department (UPD)
    Availability: Operational 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. 

Key Features & Services
    Public Safety Hub: This is where you go for non-emergency police assistance, to file a report, or to pick up a physical copy of the Annual Security and Fire Safety Report.
    Specialized Units: The building houses a Special Operations Unit for follow-up investigations and a Safety Ranger Unit that assists with general campus security measures.
    Communication Center: UPD monitors the campus's 262 "Code Blue" emergency telephones and various surveillance systems from this location. 

Notable Facts
    Accreditation: The department is a fully accredited law enforcement agency, staffed by sworn State law enforcement officers.
    Strategic Location: It is situated near the northwest entrance of the university, providing quick access to both the campus core and major surrounding roads like I-295.
    The Namesake: Named after Martin P. Garris, the building honors a legacy of campus service and safety leadership.
