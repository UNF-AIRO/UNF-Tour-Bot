Coggin College of Business, Building 42

Building 42 is the home of the Coggin College of Business, a nationally ranked business school. This 60,000-square-foot facility is designed to provide students with a hands-on learning environment modeled after the modern business world.

Core Details
    Official Name: Coggin College of Business
    Location: Centrally located adjacent to the Thomas G. Carpenter Library (Building 12). 

Key Features & Services
    High-Tech Learning: The building features 14 classrooms, an auditorium, three business technology classrooms, and five student skills labs.
    The Trading Room: A standout feature is the specialized Trading Room, where student investment analysts from the Osprey Financial Group manage a portion of the UNF endowment using Bloomberg Terminals.
    Career Management Center: Offers exclusive internship and career services specifically for Coggin students to help them network with major Jacksonville business partners.
    Academic Advising: The Coggin Office of Academic Advising is located on the second floor, helping both undergraduate and graduate students navigate their degree paths. 

Notable Facts
    The Benefactor: Named in 2002 in honor of Luther W. Coggin, founder of the Coggin Automotive Group and a major philanthropist who has provided numerous scholarships and fellowships to UNF students.
    Expansion Project: An exciting multi-phase expansion is currently underway! This project will add over 21,000 square feet of new space, connecting Building 42 with Building 10. It will include the Crowley Center for Transportation and Logistics, new research labs, and the Stellar Student Lounge, with completion expected by fall 2026.
    Global Recognition: A bot would be proud to know that Coggin is among the elite business institutions worldwide, with its MBA and Global MBA programs frequently ranked as "Tier One" by CEO Magazine.
