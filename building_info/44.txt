Fine Arts Center Parking Garage, Building 44

Building 44 is the Fine Arts Center Parking Garage, a key parking facility that supports the university’s vibrant arts district. Like the Arena Garage we saw earlier, this structure is designed to handle high volumes of traffic for both daily classes and major nightly performances.

Core Details
    Official Name: Fine Arts Center Parking Garage (often called Garage 44).
    Capacity: A four-story, covered facility designed to accommodate faculty, staff, students, and theater patrons. 

Key Features & Services
    The Covered Walkway: A standout feature for our tour—the third level of the garage features a covered pedestrian bridge that connects directly to the Fine Arts Center (Building 45). This allows performers and patrons to enter the theater without ever stepping out into the Florida rain.
    Permit Sections: While the first floor is typically reserved for specific permits, the 2nd, 3rd, and 4th floors generally serve as Blue (Student) and Gray permit lots.
    Garage Annex (44A): To keep up with demand, a newer section known as Building 44A was added. It’s accessible from the 3rd floor of the main garage and provides even more parking spaces for the campus. 

Notable Facts
    Patron Hub: This is the primary parking destination for anyone attending a Broadway-style show or concert at the Lazzara Performance Hall.
    Artistic Proximity: It is perfectly situated in the "Arts Corner" of campus, surrounded by the Fine Arts Center, the Ceramics Lab, and the Photo Lab.
    Accessibility: For visitors coming from the main campus entrance, this is often the very first parking structure they encounter on their right.
