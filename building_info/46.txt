Hodges Stadium, Building 46

Building 46 is Hodges Stadium, a premier multi-purpose athletic venue and one of the finest track and field facilities in the Southeast. It serves as the home for UNF's soccer, track and field, and cross-country teams.

Core Details
    Official Name: Hodges Stadium
    Capacity: Features a seating capacity of 9,400, making it one of the largest on-campus venues.
Key Features & Services
    The Browning Athletic Center: This 10,000-square-foot facility inside the stadium houses locker rooms, a high-tech weight room, and administrative offices for the coaching staff.
    The Track: Known as the William F. Sheffield Regional Track, it is one of only a few tracks in the U.S. certified by World Athletics (IAAF), allowing it to host international-level competitions.
    The Field: A lighted, Olympic-sized soccer pitch sits at the center of the track, hosting high-stakes ASUN Conference matches.
Notable Facts
    The Benefactors: Named in 2007 for George and Elena Hodges, whose generous $2 million gift helped transform the stadium into a world-class facility.
    National Spotlight: Because of its elite track certification, Hodges Stadium frequently hosts the NCAA Track and Field East Preliminary and the Florida Striders events, bringing thousands of visitors to Jacksonville.
    NFL Tie-in: The stadium has served as a training site for NFL teams, including the Philadelphia Eagles, when they traveled to Jacksonville for games.
