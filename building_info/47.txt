Science and Engineering Building, Building 47

Building 47 is the Science and Engineering Building, a massive 116,000-square-foot facility that serves as a cornerstone for STEM education at UNF. It is designed to foster collaboration between the College of Arts and Sciences and the School of Engineering.

Core Details
    Official Name: Science and Engineering Building
    Location: Situated in the academic core, near the J.B. Coxwell Amphitheater and the Student Union.
Key Features & Services
    Interdisciplinary Labs: The building is packed with specialized labs, including the Materials Science and Engineering Lab, Hydraulic Lab, and Environmental Engineering Lab.
    The Physics & Biology Hub: It houses the main offices and research spaces for the Department of Physics and the Department of Biology.
    Greenhouse Facilities: On the roof, you’ll find specialized greenhouse spaces used by biology students for botanical research and plant physiology studies.
    The Darwin Cafe: Located on the first floor, this is a favorite student spot to grab a quick coffee or snack between lab sessions.
Notable Facts
    Eco-Friendly Design: Like many modern UNF buildings, it was built with sustainability in mind, featuring energy-efficient systems and natural lighting to reduce its carbon footprint.
    Engineering Showcase: The first-floor hallways often feature glass-walled labs, allowing passersby to see engineering students working on concrete testing or robotics projects.
    The Courtyard Connection: It shares a beautiful outdoor courtyard with the Social Sciences Building (Building 51), providing a peaceful green space for students to study outdoors.
