UNF Golf Complex at the Hayt Learning Center, Building 48

Building 48 is the UNF Golf Complex, a premier practice and learning facility located on the eastern edge of the campus. It serves as both a training ground for the UNF Men's and Women's Golf Teams and a public-access golf center for the Jacksonville community.

Core Details
    Official Name: UNF Golf Complex at the Hayt Learning Center
    Address: 1 Betty Holzendorf Drive, Building 48
    Availability: Open to the public 7 days a week, typically from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM (with extended hours until 10:00 PM on most weeknights). 
Key Features & Services
    Practice Facilities: Includes a lighted driving range with all-weather synthetic mats and grass hitting areas, as well as several large practice putting and chipping greens.
    The "Shoe": A unique three-hole executive course (often called "The Shoe") that allows players to practice short-game scenarios in a real-course setting.
    Hayt Learning Center: The building itself houses a pro shop, club repair services, and the John Hayt Golf Center, which features indoor hitting bays and video swing analysis technology.
    PGA Instruction: Offers professional golf lessons and clinics for all skill levels, from beginners to advanced collegiate athletes.
Notable Facts
    The Namesake: Named after John Hayt, a local businessman and philanthropist whose $2 million donation was the largest in UNF Athletics history at the time, funding the facility's construction and ongoing excellence.
    Recent Renovations: The complex recently invested over $100,000 into course improvements, including new sod for the greens and upgraded artificial turf for the range, aiming to maintain its status as a top-tier practice spot.
    Student Perks: While open to the public, UNF students receive exclusive discounts on range buckets and course play, making it a popular spot for Ospreys to unwind between classes.
