UNF Preschool, Building 49

Building 49 is the UNF Preschool, a specialized early childhood center that serves as a "living laboratory" for the university. Originally known as the Child Development Research Center (CDRC), it provides high-quality care for young children while supporting academic research for UNF students and faculty.

Core Details
    Official Name: UNF Preschool
    Ages Served: Children aged 2.5 to 5 years old (must be fully potty trained).
    Affiliation: Operates under the Silverfield College of Education and Human Services.
Key Features & Services
    STEAM Curriculum: The school follows a Reggio-Inspired model with a heavy focus on STEAM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Math), encouraging children to explore and tinker in a hands-on environment.
    The STEP Lab: A unique space where preschoolers can see modern technology like 3D printers and robots, helping build the foundations for coding and engineering skills at an early age.
    Priority Enrollment: While open to the general public, enrollment is prioritized for UNF Students, followed by Faculty, Staff, and Alumni.
    Nature Integration: Its location allows children to frequently explore the university’s Nature Trails and Ogier Gardens, making the 1,400-acre campus their extended classroom.
Notable Facts
    Gold Standard Accreditation: The center is accredited by the NAEYC (National Association for the Education of Young Children), a distinction held by only a small percentage of preschools nationwide.
    A Training Ground: It serves as a vital training site for UNF students majoring in education, psychology, and health, who often conduct supervised observations and internships here.
    Community Lifeline: For student-parents, the preschool is often a "savior," providing a safe, on-campus learning environment that allows them to attend classes while their children are just a few buildings away.
