Physical Facilities, Building 5

Building 5 at the University of North Florida is known as the Physical Facilities building. It serves as the operational nerve center for the university’s maintenance and infrastructure.

Primary Function: Operations, maintenance, and campus infrastructure management.

Key Departments & Services
    Building 5 houses the teams responsible for keeping the campus running 24/7. Key services coordinated from this location include:
    Maintenance & Operations: Manages the campus "Physical Plant," which includes chilled and hot water distribution for climate control across 2.25 million square feet of building space.
    Custodial Services: Responsible for the routine and emergency cleaning of university spaces, excluding housing.
    Service Requests: This is the central hub for submitting work orders for repairs, painting, and specialty cleaning like pressure washing or carpet care.
    Emergency Infrastructure: During business hours (7 a.m. – 4 p.m.), this building handles campus facility emergencies. 
Notable Facts
    Scale of Operation: The physical plant managed here consumes electrical energy equivalent to roughly 2,000 average homes and uses five 1,000-ton chillers to cool the campus.
    Historical Note: While "Building 5" is the Physical Facilities building, historical archives sometimes refer to other early campus structures by letters or numbers during construction phases (such as the original "Osprey Village" or "Osprey Hall" construction), but in the current permanent numbering system, Building 5 is exclusively Physical Facilities.
