University of North Florida - Building 50

The Science and Engineering Building
Dedicated in 2004 as part of the events celebrating the inauguration of UNF’s fifth president, John Delaney.
The 130,000 square-foot facility includes state-of-the-art equipment essential for preparing tomorrow’s engineering, science and technology leaders.

Department of Chemistry and Biochemistry
The Department of Chemistry, headquartered in the Science and Engineering Building (Building 50), in Room 3500, serves UNF students in both general education/prerequisite courses and in three bachelor of science degree tracks accredited by the American Chemical Society.
Teaching and research laboratories give students hands-on access to modern instrumentation and techniques.

Physics Labs 1
The lab facilities offered by the Department of Physics include an extraordinary array of major research instruments valued at more than $3 million. Equipment includes a SQUID Magnetometer, a high-resolution far-infrared spectrometer, a scanning electron microscope, a Femtosecond Pulsed Laser, two atomic force microscopes and thermal and electron beam thin film decomposition systems.

Department of Physics
The Department of Physics serves students in a general education capacity as well as providing advanced instruction and research opportunities to those interested in exploring this field in a full degree program.
Proximity to engineering applications and laboratories is important for physics students and faculty.
