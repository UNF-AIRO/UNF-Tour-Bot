Social Sciences Building, Building 51

Building 51 is the Social Sciences Building, a modern four-story facility that serves as the intellectual home for several of the largest departments within the College of Arts and Sciences. It is designed to be a highly collaborative space where students study human behavior, society, and culture.

Core Details
    Official Name: Social Sciences Building
    Location: Anchors the southern end of the "STEM-Social Science" courtyard, right across from the Science and Engineering Building (Building 47).

Key Features & Services
    Major Departments: Houses the main offices for Psychology, Sociology, Anthropology, Social Work, and Political Science & Public Administration.
    Specialized Labs: Features unique research spaces, including the Psychology Testing Labs and the Archaeology Lab, where students analyze artifacts from local Florida excavation sites.
    Digital Classrooms: Equipped with high-tech seminar rooms and classrooms specifically designed for data analysis and social research presentations.
    The Courtyard Café: While technically between buildings, the outdoor seating area shared with Building 47 is a prime spot for "cross-pollination" between science and social science students.

Notable Facts
    LEED Gold Certified: This building is a champion of sustainability! It earned LEED Gold certification for its use of recycled materials, energy-efficient lighting, and "green" roofing technologies.
    The "Fishbowl" Effect: The building features massive glass walls in its central stairwell and common areas, offering stunning views of the campus woods and the nearby Peace Plaza.
    Art in Public Places: You’ll find various student and faculty art installations throughout the hallways, reflecting the building's mission to explore the human experience.
