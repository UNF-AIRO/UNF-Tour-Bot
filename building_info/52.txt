Parking Services Building, Building 52
    Building 52 is the Parking Services Building, a vital stop for anyone navigating the campus by car. It serves as the administrative headquarters for UNF Parking and Transportation Services and is often the first point of contact for visitors. 

Core Details
    Official Name: Parking Services Building (also known as the Parking Information Booth).
    Location: Conveniently situated at the main entrance to the core campus on UNF Drive.
    Availability: Staff are available to assist with parking needs, though many services are now accessible via the online parking portal. 

Key Features & Services
    Visitor Hub: This is the primary location for guests to purchase visitor parking permits and receive directions to specific buildings or event venues.
    Permit Administration: The central office for managing all student, faculty, and staff parking accounts, including the registration of vehicle license plates for the university’s virtual permit system.
    Shuttle Oversight: Serves as the command center for the Osprey Connector shuttle system, which provides transportation across the 1,600-acre campus.
    Mobility Support: Students with temporary mobility needs (like a sports injury) can visit this building to coordinate shuttle pickups or accessible parking accommodations. 

Notable Facts
    24/7 Kiosk: While the office has standard business hours, there is an automated kiosk located just outside the building where visitors can purchase daily permits at any time.
    Information First: It is strategically placed so that every car entering the campus loop passes it, ensuring that lost visitors can easily pull over for a campus map or guidance.
    Award-Winning Service: The staff here are known for their efficiency in managing thousands of permits annually while maintaining the university's "Osprey-friendly" atmosphere.
