Ann and David Hicks Hall, Building 53

Building 53 is the Ann and David Hicks Hall, a massive 155,000-square-foot facility that serves as the "Front Door" to the university. Located on the eastern edge of campus (just off I-295 and First Coast Tech Parkway), it is a unique hybrid of high-level administrative offices and academic spaces.

Core Details
    Official Name: Ann and David Hicks Hall
    Purpose: Houses the One-Stop Student Services, the Graduate School, and several key executive administrative offices.

Key Features & Services
    One-Stop Student Services: This is the most important room for students! Located on the first floor, it handles Admissions, Financial Aid, and Records/Registration all in one spot.
    The Graduate School: The second floor is the headquarters for UNF Graduate Programs, providing support for students pursuing master’s and doctoral degrees.
    Human Resources & Controller: It houses the university’s "behind-the-scenes" engines, including the Human Resources department and the Controller’s Office (Student Accounts).
    Hicks Honors College: The prestigious Hicks Honors College administrative offices are located here, overseeing specialized curriculum and global study opportunities.

Notable Facts
    The Transformation: This building wasn't always a part of UNF; it was originally the headquarters for AOL (America Online) before the university purchased and renovated it in 2009.
    The Namesake: Named after Ann and David Hicks, prominent Jacksonville philanthropists whose $10 million gift established a scholarship endowment for students living in public housing.
    The Bridge to Campus: Because it sits slightly apart from the main campus loop, it is a major stop for the Osprey Connector shuttle, which runs back and forth to the core campus every few minutes.
    Art Gallery: The lobby often features rotating art exhibits and serves as a sophisticated venue for university-wide receptions and donor events.
