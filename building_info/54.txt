Osprey Fountains, Building 54

Building 54 is the Osprey Fountains, the crown jewel of UNF’s residential life. This massive, five-story, 385,000-square-foot luxury residence hall houses approximately 1,000 students and feels more like a resort than a traditional dorm.

Core Details
    Official Name: Osprey Fountains
    Housing Type: Private and semi-private suites (primarily for upperclassmen, though some freshmen sections exist).
    Location: Situated on the "Eastern Ridge," connected to the core campus by a scenic pedestrian bridge over the university's nature preserve.

Key Features & Services
    The Upper Deck: A massive recreational area featuring a beach-entry swimming pool, a lazy river, a putting green, and lighted volleyball and basketball courts.
    The Galaxy Diner: A 1950s-themed on-site diner where students can grab milkshakes, burgers, and breakfast using their meal plans.
    Fitness & Fun: Includes its own private fitness center (The Ozone), a massive game room (The Hub), and specialized themed lounges like the "Library Lounge" for quiet study or the "Social Lounge" with a plasma-screen theater.
    The Bridge: Residents enjoy a private, lighted walkway that cuts through the woods, offering a peaceful 10-minute walk or a quick bike ride to the Student Union.

Notable Facts
    Award-Winning Design: When it opened in 2009, it was hailed as one of the most innovative student housing projects in the country, earning LEED Silver certification for its sustainable construction.
    The "Vibe": It’s famous for its themed "houses" within the building (e.g., The Arts House, The Wellness House), allowing students with similar interests to live together.
    Osprey Connector: Because it’s a bit of a hike from the library, the Osprey Connector shuttle has a dedicated stop right at the front door, running every few minutes during the day.
