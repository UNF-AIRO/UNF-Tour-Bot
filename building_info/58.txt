John A. Delaney Student Union, Building 58
    Building 58 is the John A. Delaney Student Union, the vibrant "living room" of the UNF campus. This award-winning, two-building complex (East and West) is the central hub for student life, dining, and campus events.

Core Details
    Official Name: John A. Delaney Student Union
    Location: Centrally located at the heart of campus, adjacent to the Library and the "Green."

Key Features & Services
    The Food Court: The East Building (58E) is a foodie’s paradise, featuring Chick-fil-A, Panda Express, Chop'd & Wrap'd, and Crust.
    Student Government & Clubs: The West Building (58W) houses the Student Government chambers and offices for over 200 student organizations.
    The UNF Bookstore: A massive two-story shop where you can grab your textbooks, the latest Osprey gear, or a snack at the in-store Starbucks.
    The Boathouse: A legendary UNF tradition! This on-campus restaurant and hangout spot overlooks the lake and is the go-to place for wings, trivia nights, and live music.
    Market Days: Every Wednesday, the Plaza between the two buildings transforms into a bustling marketplace with local vendors, music, and club tables. 

Notable Facts
    The Namesake: Named after John A. Delaney, UNF’s longest-serving president (2003–2018) and a former Mayor of Jacksonville.
    Architecture Award: Shortly after opening in 2009, it was voted the #1 building in Florida by the American Institute of Architects (AIA) in their "100 Years, 100 Places" competition.
    Eco-Friendly: The complex is LEED Gold certified, featuring a high-tech "green" roof and natural lighting that reduces energy consumption.
    Game Center: The bottom floor of the West building features the Osprey Game Center, packed with pool tables, ping pong, and gaming consoles for students to de-stress.
