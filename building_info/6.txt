Building 6, UNF Annex

Building 6 is officially known as the UNF Annex. Like Building 5, it is a one-story structure focused on the "behind-the-scenes" operations that keep the university running, while also providing unique specialized lab spaces. 

Core Details
    Official Name: UNF Annex
    Primary Function: Mixed-use space for administrative services and specialized academic labs.

Key Departments & Services
    The UNF Annex houses several diverse units that don't fit into a standard classroom building: 
    Environmental Health and Safety (EH&S): Located in Suite 1300, this department handles campus safety, insurance, risk management, and building code administration.
    Sculpture Lab: Part of the Art, Art History, & Design Department, this specialized facility allows students to work on large-scale art projects.
    CCEC Labs: It houses laboratories for the College of Computing, Engineering and Construction, providing hands-on space for technical projects.
    Administrative Support: Staff here are responsible for various smooth-operation tasks for the university infrastructure.

Historical Note
Numbering Confusion: In older university records (circa 1985), the number "6" was sometimes associated with Osprey Village housing buildings during their initial development phases. However, in the current permanent campus map, Building 6 refers strictly to the UNF Annex.
