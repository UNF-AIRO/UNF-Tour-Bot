Alumni Hall, Building 60

Building 60 is Alumni Hall, a specialized administrative center located just off the campus core. It serves as the primary bridge between the university and its global network of over 100,000 graduates, donors, and community partners. 

Core Details
    Official Name: Alumni Hall.
    Primary Function: Administrative headquarters for university advancement and community engagement. 

Key Features & Services
    Alumni Association: Home to the UNF Alumni Association, which coordinates events, networking opportunities, and career services for former students.
    Annual Giving: Houses the offices for Annual Giving and Advancement Services, managing the university's fundraising efforts and donor relations.
    Community Engagement: Serves as a hub for "friend-raising" activities, supporting the university's mission to build strong ties with the Jacksonville community. 

Notable Facts
    Strategic Separation: Unlike the busy student-centered Student Union (Building 58), Alumni Hall is positioned to provide a professional and welcoming environment specifically for visiting alumni and donors.
    Alumni Square: It is located near Alumni Square, a popular outdoor courtyard featuring mosaic artwork designed by UNF students and faculty, serving as a symbolic link between current students and those who came before them.
    Milestone Marker: The establishment of the Alumni Association in 1976 was a major milestone in UNF's history, and this building represents the permanent home for that growing legacy.
