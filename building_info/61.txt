Student Wellness Complex, Building 61

Building 61 is the Student Wellness Complex, a massive 76,000-square-foot facility dedicated to the health and fitness of the Osprey community. Since its grand opening in 2012, it has become one of the most popular spots on campus for students to blow off steam and stay active.

Core Details
    Official Name: Student Wellness Complex
    Primary Occupant: Department of Recreation and Wellness.

Key Features & Services
    The Paul Shirley Fitness Center: Spanning two floors, this 27,000-square-foot fitness floor is packed with over 200 pieces of cardio and strength-training equipment.
    The Dottie Dorion Fitness Ramp: A unique, 1/8-mile indoor track that wraps around the perimeter of the fitness floor, allowing you to run or walk while overlooking the entire gym.
    The Climbing Wall: A centerpiece of the lobby, this 34-foot custom-built rock climbing wall features routes for everyone from beginners to expert climbers.
    Group Fitness Studios: Home to yoga, Zumba, HIIT, and cycling classes, all of which are typically free for current students.
    The Osprey Cliff: An outdoor bouldering wall for those who want to practice their climbing skills in the Florida sun.

Notable Facts
    LEED Gold Certified: Keeping with UNF's "green" mission, the building features recycled rubber flooring, energy-efficient lighting, and a design that maximizes natural Florida sunlight.
    Student-Funded: This $19.5 million complex was funded primarily by student fees, meaning the students literally built their own world-class gym!
    Top-Tier Ranking: It is frequently ranked as one of the best campus recreation centers in the nation for its modern design and comprehensive wellness programs.
