Housing Maintenance Building, Building 62

Building 62 is the Housing Maintenance Building, the operational heart of the university’s massive residential system. While it may not have the luxury pools of the Fountains or the high-tech labs of the science buildings, it is arguably the most important facility for the thousands of students living on campus. 

Core Details
    Official Name: Housing Maintenance Building
    Primary Occupant: UNF Housing and Residence Life maintenance and facility staff. 

Key Features & Services
    Maintenance HQ: This is the central workshop and dispatch center for all housing-related repairs, from fixing leaky faucets to managing electrical issues across all residence halls.
    Service Requests: While students submit Maintenance Service Requests online, this building is where those orders are processed and where the specialized tools and supplies are stored to keep the dorms running smoothly.
    Lockshop Services: The housing lockshop is often based out of or coordinated through this area, assisting with key replacements and electronic lock maintenance for student rooms. 

Notable Facts
    24/7 Behind-the-Scenes: Although the building itself isn't a public "hangout," the staff operating out of Building 62 are on call to handle emergency maintenance issues 24 hours a day.
    Support for Growth: As UNF has added massive communities like the Osprey Fountains and The Flats, this facility has expanded its operations to manage the increased demand for residential upkeep.
    Strategic Location: It is positioned near the Campus Maintenance Facility (Building 64) on the eastern side of the campus, tucked away from the main academic walk to allow easy access for service vehicles.
