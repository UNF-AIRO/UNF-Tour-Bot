Campus Maintenance Facility, Building 64

Building 64 is the Campus Maintenance Facility, the command center for the UNF Physical Facilities department. If Building 63 is the annex, then 64 is the brain—it's where the university’s massive infrastructure is monitored, managed, and repaired 24 hours a day.

Core Details
    Official Name: Campus Maintenance Facility
    Primary Occupant: UNF Physical Facilities Administration, Engineering, and Trade Shops.

Key Features & Services
    Central Dispatch: This is the headquarters for the university's "Help Desk" for physical issues. Whether it’s a power outage in a lab or a broken AC in a dorm, the work orders are dispatched from right here.
    Trade Specialized Shops: The building houses high-capacity workshops for Electricians, HVAC Technicians, Plumbers, and Locksmiths. These teams maintain over 4 million square feet of building space across the campus.
    Energy Management Center: A high-tech "war room" where staff monitor the university’s energy consumption in real-time, adjusting cooling and lighting systems across campus to save money and reduce UNF's carbon footprint.

Notable Facts
    Storm Command: During Florida’s hurricane season, Building 64 becomes the "Hurricane Command Center." The essential personnel stationed here are responsible for boarding up buildings, clearing fallen trees, and ensuring the campus is safe for students to return.
    Sustainability HQ: This is where the magic of UNF’s "Green" initiative happens. The staff here manage the recycled water irrigation system and the transition to LED lighting across all 1,600 acres.
    Strategic Location: Tucked away on the eastern edge of the main loop, it allows heavy service trucks to move in and out of campus without disrupting student traffic or the "pedestrian-only" zones in the campus core.
