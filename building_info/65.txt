The Flats at UNF, Building 65

Building 65 is The Flats at UNF, a modern, apartment-style housing community that offers students a bridge between on-campus convenience and off-campus independence. Located just across Kernan Boulevard from the main campus entrance, it is the premier choice for upperclassmen and graduate students.

Core Details
    Official Name: The Flats at UNF
    Housing Type: Fully furnished 1, 2, and 3-bedroom apartments with full kitchens and private bathrooms for every bedroom.
    Target Audience: Primarily reserved for upperclassmen (60+ credits), transfer students, and graduate students.

Key Features & Services
    Resort-Style Living: The complex features its own private clubhouse, a sparkling swimming pool, a 24-hour fitness center, and a sand volleyball court.
    The "All-In" Advantage: Unlike off-campus apartments, the rent at The Flats includes all utilities (electricity, water, internet) and is billed directly to the student’s UNF account, allowing for the use of financial aid.
    On-Site Management: While it feels like an independent apartment, it is managed by UNF Housing and Residence Life, meaning you still have access to Resident Assistants (RAs) and 24/7 maintenance.
    Private Shuttle Stop: A dedicated Osprey Connector shuttle stop is located right at the front gate, whisking students to the core campus in under 10 minutes.

Notable Facts
    The Acquisition: Originally built as a private apartment complex called "The Flats at Kernan," UNF purchased the property in 2014 to meet the massive demand for upperclassman housing.
    Pet Friendly: Unlike most traditional dorms, The Flats is one of the few places on campus where students can apply to live with a registered pet.
    Nature Trails: The back of the property connects directly to the UNF Nature Trails, giving residents instant access to miles of hiking and biking paths through the campus sanctuary.
