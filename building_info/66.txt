Cooper Beach Volleyball Courts, Building 66

While not a traditional "building" in the sense of classrooms or offices, Building 66 refers to the premier sand volleyball facility on campus. It is the competitive home for the UNF Beach Volleyball team and a popular recreation spot for students. 

Core Details
    Official Name: Cooper Beach Volleyball Courts.
    Location: Situated on the northwest side of campus, near Hodges Stadium (Building 46) and the Harmon Baseball Stadium (Building 27). 

Key Features & Services
    Competition Venue: Features five professional-grade sand courts that meet NCAA standards for intercollegiate play.
    Spectator Areas: Designed with tiered seating and viewing areas for fans to watch home matches during the spring season.
    Student Recreation: When not in use for official team practices or matches, the courts are often available for student use through UNF Recreation and Wellness. 

Notable Facts
    The Namesake: Named after The Cooper Family, longtime supporters of UNF Athletics whose generosity helped elevate the beach volleyball program to national prominence.
    Elite Surface: The facility uses a specific type of high-quality, fine-grain sand designed to stay cool under the Florida sun and provide the ideal surface for high-level athletic performance.
    Athletic Hub: Its inclusion in the "Athletics Corridor" means it’s just steps away from other major sports facilities, making it easy for fans to catch multiple Osprey games in a single afternoon.
