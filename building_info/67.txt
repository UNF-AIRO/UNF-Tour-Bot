Osprey Ridge, Building 67

Building 67 is Osprey Ridge, the newest residence hall on the UNF campus. Having officially opened for the Fall 2025 semester, it represents the first major housing expansion at the university since 2009. 

Core Details
    Official Name: Osprey Ridge
    Capacity: A massive 165,000-square-foot, four-story facility housing approximately 520 students.
    Location: Situated on Osprey Ridge Road, directly across from Osprey Fountains (Building 54). 

Key Features & Services
    Honors Home: It serves as the primary home for the Hicks Honors College community, though it is open to all students.
    Modern Amenities: Includes specialized study lounges, kitchenettes, a convenience store, and a catering room.
    Outdoor Recreation: The facility features its own pickleball and sand volleyball courts surrounded by scenic green space.
    The Boardwalk: A brand-new scenic boardwalk connects the building directly to the main campus core, providing a beautiful walk through the nature preserve. 

Notable Facts
    Living-Learning Communities (LLCs): Starting in 2025, it hosts the Honors First-Year LLC, Global Honors Theme Community, and Honors Horizons, allowing like-minded students to live and study together.
    Sustainable Design: The building was designed by PBK and built by Ajax Building Company, incorporating modern architectural standards for student success and collaboration.
    Phase Two: Early planning is already underway for an adjacent academic center to further support the Honors community.
