Roy Lassiter Hall, Building 8

Building 8 at the University of North Florida is officially known as Roy Lassiter Hall. It serves as a central academic and business hub for students, combining essential administrative services with classroom and dining facilities.

Core Details
    Official Name: Roy Lassiter Hall
    Former Name: It was previously known as English Hall before being renamed in 2017.
    Primary Function: A multi-use academic and student business center.
    Location: Centrally located on campus, situated between the Student Union and the Library.

Key Departments & Services: Lassiter Hall is one of the most visited buildings due to the essential student services it houses:
    Osprey 1Card Office: Located in Suite 1100, this is where students and staff go to get their official university ID cards.
    Department of English: The building remains the primary home for English faculty offices and the Writing Program.
    Business Services: Houses the university's Business Services administration, the Property Department, and the Ticket Box Office.
    Student Amenities: Includes a Chick-fil-A express and Osprey Print and Ship Services (UPS Store).
    Specialized Labs: Features a Language Computer Lab and the AfterImage Film Lab.

Historical Significance
    Construction: The building was first constructed in 1974.
    Namesake: It was dedicated in February 2017 to honor Roy Lassiter Jr., a founding faculty member and the university's first Vice President for Academic Affairs.
    Naming Evolution: Originally an Arts and Sciences facility, it was often referred to as the English Hall for decades before its official dedication.
